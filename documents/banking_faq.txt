BANKING FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1. What is KYC?

KYC means Know Your Customer. It is a process used by banks to verify the identity and address of customers.


Q2. What is a savings account?

A savings account is a bank account used to store money and earn interest.


Q3. What is a current account?

A current account is generally used by businesses and organizations for frequent transactions.


Q4. What is a personal loan?

A personal loan is a loan that can be used for personal expenses such as education, medical expenses, travel, weddings, or home requirements.


Q5. What documents are required for a personal loan?

Common documents include Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, address proof, salary slips, and bank account statements.


Q6. What is an EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount paid by a borrower every month towards a loan.


Q7. What is a Fixed Deposit?

A Fixed Deposit is a deposit where money is kept with a bank for a fixed period and earns interest.


Q8. What is a Recurring Deposit?

A Recurring Deposit allows a customer to deposit a fixed amount regularly, usually every month, for a selected period.


Q9. What is a Home Loan?

A home loan is a loan used to purchase, construct, or renovate a house.


Q10. What is an Education Loan?

An education loan helps students pay for higher education expenses.


Q11. What is a Car Loan?

A car loan is used to finance the purchase of a new or used vehicle.


Q12. What is a Credit Score?

A credit score is a numerical indicator used by financial institutions to assess a person's credit history and creditworthiness.


Q13. What is a Bank Account?

A bank account is an account maintained with a bank to deposit, withdraw, and manage money.


Q14. What is a Bank Statement?

A bank statement is a record of transactions made through a bank account during a specific period.


Q15. What is an ATM?

ATM stands for Automated Teller Machine. It allows customers to perform certain banking transactions such as cash withdrawal and balance checking.
