BANK ACCOUNT INFORMATION

Savings Account

A savings account is a bank account used to safely store money and earn interest.

Basic Requirements:
- Applicant should provide identity proof.
- Applicant should provide address proof.
- Applicant should complete KYC verification.

Common Documents:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Passport-size Photograph
- Address Proof

Current Account

A current account is mainly used by businesses and organizations for frequent transactions.

Common Requirements:
- Identity Proof
- Address Proof
- PAN Card
- Business Registration Documents
- KYC Documents


Fixed Deposit

A Fixed Deposit, also called FD, allows customers to deposit money for a fixed period and earn interest.

Important Points:
- The customer chooses a deposit amount.
- The customer chooses a fixed tenure.
- Interest is paid according to the applicable bank rate.
- Premature withdrawal may be subject to bank rules.


Recurring Deposit

A Recurring Deposit, also called RD, allows customers to deposit a fixed amount regularly for a selected period.

Important Points:
- A fixed amount is deposited every month.
- Interest is earned according to the applicable bank rate.
- The maturity amount depends on the deposit amount, tenure, and interest rate.


KYC

KYC means Know Your Customer.

KYC is used by banks to verify the identity and address of customers.

Common KYC Documents:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Passport
- Driving Licence
- Voter ID
