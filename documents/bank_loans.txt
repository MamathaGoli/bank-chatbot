BANK LOAN INFORMATION

Personal Loan

A personal loan can be used for personal expenses such as education, medical expenses, travel, weddings, or home requirements.

Basic Eligibility:
- Age should be between 21 and 60 years.
- Applicant should have a regular source of income.
- Applicant should have a valid identity and address proof.
- Applicant should have a good credit history.

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Address Proof
- Salary Slips
- Bank Account Statement

Example Personal Loan:
Loan Amount: ₹3,00,000
Interest Rate: 10% per year
Loan Tenure: 5 years

The actual interest rate and eligibility may vary depending on the applicant and bank policies.


Home Loan

A home loan is used to purchase, construct, or renovate a house.

Basic Eligibility:
- Applicant should be at least 21 years old.
- Applicant should have a regular income.
- Applicant should have valid identity and address proof.
- Applicant should meet the bank's credit requirements.

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Income Proof
- Salary Slips
- Bank Statements
- Property Documents


Education Loan

An education loan helps students pay for higher education expenses.

Basic Eligibility:
- Student should have admission to a recognized educational institution.
- Parent or guardian may be required as a co-applicant.
- Required documents must be submitted.

Required Documents:
- Student ID
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Admission Letter
- Fee Structure
- Academic Certificates
- Parent or Guardian Income Proof


Car Loan

A car loan is used to purchase a new or used vehicle.

Basic Eligibility:
- Applicant should meet the bank's age requirement.
- Applicant should have a regular source of income.
- Applicant should have valid identity and address proof.

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Income Proof
- Bank Statement
- Vehicle Quotation
